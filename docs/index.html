Sources, method and caveats

    The same property often appears on several sites. Listings were matched by county, road name, lot number, acreage and price, so each property shows once, with a link to every site where it was found. Where sites disagree, the lower asking price is shown and the other price is noted. Each listing's county was checked against its map location.

    Under contract: listings shown as pending or contingent on Realtor.com, or under contract on LandWatch, when checked the morning of Nov 5 are not shown. “Check status” means one site shows it under contract while another still shows it for sale. Homes.com and LandSearch don't publish status, so a listing found only there is shown as active.

    Driving distance: approximate driving miles and minutes from your reference address to each map pin, using OpenStreetMap road data (OSRM routing), without traffic. For approximate pins the actual drive can differ. Your reference address is not shown on this public page; the Directions link starts from wherever you are when you open it.

    Your marks: the NO and Interested checkboxes are saved in this browser on this device (they are not uploaded anywhere), so they stay after a refresh but won't appear on another computer or phone. Clearing the browser's site data erases them.

    Photos: listing photos load directly from LandWatch and belong to the listing brokers; click one to open the listing. Where no listing photo was available, an aerial image (Esri World Imagery) of the mapped location is shown instead.

    Map pins: solid pins use the location published by the listing site. Dashed pins had no published location, so they were placed by looking up the street address (US Census geocoder) or the road name (OpenStreetMap); a road-name pin can be some distance from the actual parcel. County outlines are from the US Census. “Days listed” counts from when the listing first appeared on LandWatch, or Redfin's days on market.

    Zillow and Trulia carry the same MLS listings as Realtor.com, Homes.com and Redfin, so they were not pulled separately.
