Sources, method and caveats

    The same property often appears on several sites. Listings were matched by county, road name, lot number, acreage and price, so each property shows once, with a link to every site where it was found. Where sites disagree, the lower asking price is shown and the other price is noted.

    Search range: more than 10 and less than 50 acres, under $1,000,000, in Montgomery, Stanly, Anson, Richmond and Rowan Counties. Montgomery and Stanly were checked live on LandWatch and Realtor.com up to $750,000; from $750,000 to $1,000,000 they come from Homes.com, LandSearch and Redfin. Anson, Richmond and Rowan come from Homes.com (current) and the latest available LandWatch/Land And Farm copies for 11 to 50 acres, which may be a few days old; Realtor.com, LandSearch and Redfin were not checked for those three counties. Listing photos are available only where LandWatch was checked live; elsewhere an aerial view of the mapped spot is shown.

    "Under contract" means at least one MLS-fed site (Realtor.com or Redfin) shows it pending or contingent, or every site that lists it does. "Check status" means the sites disagree or the status could not be confirmed. Acreage, price and status are as advertised; confirm details, boundaries, access, perc/soil results and restrictions with the listing agent and the county GIS before making an offer.

    Photos: listing photos load directly from LandWatch and belong to the listing brokers; click one to open the listing. Where no listing photo was available, an aerial image (Esri World Imagery) of the mapped location is shown instead, with a ring at the pin.

    Distance: straight-line ("as the crow flies") miles from your reference address, rounded to the nearest mile. The address itself is not shown on this public page. Driving distance will be longer, especially for properties across Lake Tillery. The Directions link starts from wherever you are when you open it.

    Map pins: solid pins use the location published by the listing site. Dashed pins had no published location, so they were placed by looking up the street address (US Census geocoder) or the road name (OpenStreetMap), using a lookup helper that runs in this repository; a road-name pin can be some distance from the actual parcel. County outlines are from the US Census. "Days listed" counts from when the listing first appeared on LandWatch, or Redfin's days on market.

    Zillow and Trulia carry the same MLS listings as Realtor.com, Homes.com and Redfin, so they were not pulled separately. Land And Farm, Lands of America and Land.com share LandWatch's listing database.

    Personal research dashboard. Not affiliated with any listing site. Listing data and photos belong to the respective sites and brokers.
